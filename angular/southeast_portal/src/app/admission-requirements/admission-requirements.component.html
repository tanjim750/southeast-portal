  • Attested copies of all academic certificates and mark sheets
  • 1-8 for diploma students along with original documents
  • 4 Copies of recent photographs (passport size)
  • Student’s NID/Birth certificate and Guardian’s NID photocopy attested
  • Attested Job experience certificate (only for 48 credits MBA)
  • Foreign Students: A copy of recent passport (at least one (1) year validity) Copies of all academic documents together with a testimonial, attested by the Diplomatic Mission Stating the duration of the courses studied by the applicant. School of Science & Engineering (SSE)
Program Requirements
Business & Administration A student should have at least second division or minimum GPA of 2.5 in both SSC & HSC examinations or a minimum GPA of 2.00 in SSC or in HSC, combined total being not lower than 6.00.
. A student must have at least 5 subjects in O Level with GPA 2.50 and 2 subjects in A Level with GPA 2.0 (in the scale of A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1).
Southeast Business School (SBS)
Program Requirements
Business & Administration A student should have at least second division or minimum GPA of 2.5 in both SSC & HSC examinations or a minimum GPA of 2.00 in SSC or in HSC, combined total being not lower than 6.00.
. A student must have at least 5 subjects in O Level with GPA 2.50 and 2 subjects in A Level with GPA 2.0 (in the scale of A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1).
Program Requirements
MBA (Regular) A student should have at least second division or minimum GPA of 2.5 in both SSC & HSC examinations or a minimum GPA of 2.00 in SSC or in HSC, combined total being not lower than 6.00.
A student must have at least 5 subjects in O Level with GPA 2.50 and 2 subjects in A Level with GPA 2.0 (in the scale of A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1).
At least a Bachelor’s degree with minimum 2nd division/class or CGPA of at least 2.00.
MBA (One-Year): A student should have at least second division or minimum GPA of 2.5 in both SSC & HSC examinations or a minimum GPA of 2.00 in SSC or in HSC, combined total being not lower than 6.00.
A student must have at least 5 subjects in O Level with GPA 2.50 and 2 subjects in A Level with GPA 2.0 (in the scale of A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1).
At least a Bachelor’s degree with minimum 2nd division/class or CGPA of at least 2.00 and any job experience.
